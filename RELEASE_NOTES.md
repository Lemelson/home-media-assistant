# v0.1.0-alpha.1 — First public preview

Source-only preview of a self-hosted Telegram media-library assistant for macOS, Transmission and optional Plex. Includes EN/RU documentation, empty configuration examples, numeric chat access, durable job queues, disk UUID checks and scoped deletion confirmation.

Use only content whose permission covers downloading and any peer-to-peer distribution. No media, credentials, private deployment history, ready-made indexers or artwork are included. External voice and images are opt-in. MIT covers the provided original software, not third-party content or service rights.

Known limits: the bot UI is primarily Russian; new live installations and the CI platform matrix still need their own verification. Local tests do not certify unattended recovery, legal compliance or absence of security issues. See VALIDATION.md and the bilingual setup/privacy/legal guides.

## Первый публичный выпуск

Медиа, секреты, частная история, индексаторы и изображения не поставляются. Голос и картинки включаются отдельно.
